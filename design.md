# AI Hub, Ghana — Initial Brand Guideline (Draft)

**Draft · 26 August 2026**
**Accra · Operations from 2026**

> **Read first:** No logo exists yet, and the name is not final. The lockup ("AI HUB GHANA") is the name set in type as a stand-in — placeholder, not a logo. This sheet is a first draft to keep work consistent in the meantime; it is not the finished brand. Once the name is agreed and the identity is designed, this is replaced in full. Supersedes nothing.

Chalk (Paper) is the default ground, never white. Brick is dark: nothing darker than Paper meets contrast on it, so anything sitting on Brick is set in Paper.

## Typeface

**Mona Sans** — Google Fonts, open licence. One variable family, weight 200–900 and width 75–125, so no second font is needed.

| Style | Weight | Tracking |
|---|---|---|
| Statement | 800 caps | −0.055em |
| Heading | 700–800 | −0.03em |
| Body | 400 | 0 |
| Kicker | 800 caps | 0.18em |

Example statement styling: "THE BRIDGE BETWEEN GHANA AND EUROPE." (heading in Ink, final line in Brick).

## Shape

Pills, and one or two oversized rounded corners per shape — never all four, never a cut corner. The curve is the signature.

## Voice

Plain and specific. Caps for statements of three to six words; sentence case for anything carrying an argument. Never a paragraph in caps.

## Rules that matter

- Paper as the ground, not white.
- Real photographs of real people.
- One call to action per page.
- Colour does the dividing, not boxes.
- No gradients, glows or drop shadows.
- No stock photography.
- No AI-generated imagery.
- No marketing adjectives.
