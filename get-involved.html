Why Ghana, and why now

Four reasons, not one.

Ghana has decided to become Africa’s AI hub — and is putting real money, policy and talent behind the ambition.

01

A national commitment, not a pilot. In April 2026 Ghana launched a ten-year National AI Strategy backed by roughly USD 270 million — including USD 250 million for a national AI computing centre intended to serve research, enterprise and the wider region.

02

Talent, at scale. A young, English-speaking graduate population, and a digital services sector that already employs more than 20,000 people. Ghana’s ambition is to add 100,000 more.

03

A shortage worth solving. Germany and the DACH region have carried well over 100,000 unfilled ICT positions in recent years. Accra sits an hour or two from Frankfurt — the same working day, not the other side of the world.

04

German partnership already on the ground. Through FAIR Forward – Artificial Intelligence for All and the Invest for Jobs Special Initiative, German Development Cooperation has spent years in Ghana building AI skills, open data and policy foundations on behalf of the Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ).
